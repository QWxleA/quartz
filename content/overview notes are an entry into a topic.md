[[Zettelkasten and Evergreen notes]] 

**Overview notes** are also called **Map of Contents**. Notes that give an overview of a specific topic, containing up-to some 25 notes.

Overview notes should not get too large. The atomic principle also applies here. If it grows too large it should just be divided over multiple notes

When the initial index gets too large group several notes, that fall under one keyword on one structure note.
