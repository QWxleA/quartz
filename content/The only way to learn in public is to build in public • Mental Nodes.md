## Highlights

Our natural fear of being judged leads most people to build, learn, and think privately. But seeking validation should not be the goal of learning in public. Instead, the goal should be to tap into your network's collective intelligence to create constructive feedback loops.[🔗](https://omnivore.app/me/the-only-way-to-learn-in-public-is-to-build-in-public-mental-nod-1883cfd311d#ada4515c-411e-4f6e-9cf6-2d8b21c3d6c1)  ^ada4515c

An overused Reid Hoffman's quote captures some of the idea of learning in public: “If you are not embarrassed by the first version of your product, you've launched too late.” In practice, it means sharing what you build often and early; not waiting until a project is polished to ask for feedback; proactively seeking help and suggestions from your peers.[🔗](https://omnivore.app/me/the-only-way-to-learn-in-public-is-to-build-in-public-mental-nod-1883cfd311d#8826f1dd-41fd-4330-9bbc-9f8fe2e74241)  ^8826f1dd
