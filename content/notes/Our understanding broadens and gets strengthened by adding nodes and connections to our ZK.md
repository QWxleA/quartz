[[Zettelkasten and Evergreen MOC]] #expansion 

An idea gets written down in an [[atomic note]], with one specific context, the context of that note and understanding. When linking a note, we broaden the context by adding the two notes together. Especially of the other notes is from a (slightly) unrelated field, our understanding and application of our first note grows.

When moving ideas from a previous context in our own understanding, we have to carefully consider the meaning. We want to really understand **and** not misrepresent the original author.

As you add more notes and create more links you literally broaded your understanding. [[True understanding is understanding the limits (borders) of a text, and then having the ability to look beyond]]

## Literature notes
![[ahrens_2022#^69475f]]
>– [[How to Take Smart Notes - Sönke Ahrens]]

The nodes in our ZK, chained together form our thoughts. Adding to these nodes means expanding our thinking
- Reading material is transformed into nodes in the ZK. These connected nodes represent chains of thought. As we add nodes, and revisit chains our thinking is expanded and reformed
- The growth of the ZK represents our growth of understanding

>Spaced repetition systems are designed to facilitate this effect. If you want prompts to reinforce your understanding of some topic, you must learn to write prompts which collectively invoke retrieval practice of all the key details.
>– [[How to write good prompts- using spaced repetition to create understanding]]
