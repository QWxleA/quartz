## Zotero
Chances are pretty good your college/university's library does regular tutorials for tools like Zotero, particularly at the beginning of the term. Raul Pacheco has some good notes/ideas which may be helpful for things like literature reviews: [http://www.raulpacheco.org/resources/](http://www.raulpacheco.org/resources/). For [Hypothes.is](https://Hypothes.is), try starting with this tutorial by their head of education: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=09z5hyBMs8s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=09z5hyBMs8s). If you're using Obsidian with Zotero, I'd recommend this walk through [https://forum.obsidian.md/t/zotero-zotfile-mdnotes-obsidian-dataview-workflow/15536](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/zotero-zotfile-mdnotes-obsidian-dataview-workflow/15536) and this video [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XbGJH08ZfCs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XbGJH08ZfCs). If you have other tools in mind that you'd like to use, let the community know and perhaps we can make some suggestions about tutorials, but really, just jump in and try something out. Search YouTube and see what you find.
