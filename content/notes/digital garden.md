[[Zettelkasten and Evergreen MOC]]

>“So plant your own gardens and decorate your own soul, instead of waiting for someone to bring you flowers.” — Jorge Luis Borges

A digital garden is a personal collection of notes, carefully tended and expanded over time. As all beautiful gardens, sharing (or showing of) your garden is part of the process ([[learning in public]]).

A gardening approach to note-taking cultivates the **habit to improve and tend** ([[iteration]]) to our notes [[spaced repetition as a productivity tool]]. The [[zettelkasten]] need to interlink notes fits into this ethos as well.
