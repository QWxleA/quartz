> [!NOTE] This is a zettelkasten related note

**Context is everything in zettelkasten because ==individual notes are meaningless== without their connections and surrounding web of relationships.**

A zettelkasten note isn't designed to be self-contained like a traditional document. Instead, each note gains its significance from:

**Relational meaning**: A note about "emergence" means different things when connected to biology notes versus economics notes versus philosophy notes. The same concept takes on new dimensions based on its neighborhood.

→ [[The value of a permanent note is in its links]]

**Conversational continuity**: Notes reference ongoing threads of thought that span multiple entries. Without understanding the broader conversation, individual notes appear fragmentary or incomplete.

→ [[folgezettel]]

**Temporal context**: Ideas evolve across notes over time. A concept you explored six months ago might be refined, contradicted, or expanded in recent notes. The development arc matters.

→ [[A good note connects your current understanding with the needs of your future work]]

**Associative networks**: The value comes from unexpected intersections between seemingly unrelated domains. A note's importance often lies not in its content but in how it bridges different areas of knowledge.

→ [[Walking keywords shows multiple contexts in which we can find answers]]

**Emergent properties**: Patterns and insights emerge from the collection that don't exist in any single note. The whole becomes greater than its parts only through contextual relationships.

→ [[keywords open new doors, conversation starters for a note]]
→ [[Walking keywords shows multiple contexts in which we can find answers]]

Without this contextual web, you're left with isolated fragments rather than a living knowledge system. The connections ARE the knowledge - not just pointers to it.

→ [[note sequences are the key to ZK, they create clusters that birth new ideas]]
