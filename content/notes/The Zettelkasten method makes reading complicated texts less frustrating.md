Reading a book or a paper one might feel forced to read & understand everything, but that is not needed. There is **too much information** in the world, so one has to choose anyway.

Getting the freedom to choose makes reading nicer. Picking and choosing the best parts is like only eating desert.
→ [[Who rebound books and removed parts?]]

With AI tools it is possible to ask which chapters are the “best”. Too many books, too little time.
→ [[AI prompts before reading a book]]

Then, the good parts can be moved into your notes. Key is to think about the parts you do read. [[collectors fallacy]] is a risk.
