[[⏱️Being productive and healthy]] #planning  → #structure  → #process 

Longer and more complicated tasks cannot be finished on initial enthusiasm alone. In time those will wane, as the task is no longer interesting and new. [[Fear of missing out]] and [[shiny toy syndrome|next shiny thing]]  will ask for our attention. Having workflows in place that we enjoy will reduce pain points. Combined with triggers of how much work is still to be done ([[Ovsiankina effect]]), might trick us into continueing work, or at least continueing at a later date.

A satisfactory workflow should not feel like flogging oneself. Willpower alone is not enough to power through *everything* ([[Willpower as a limited resource]]). It should give the freedom to go "do something else". The system should make sure nothing gets forgotten or left behind ([[spaced repetition as a productivity tool]]). Which help to [[Trust the system, and learn to let go]].

Feedback within the [[workflow|workflow]] will make completing more enjoyable (eg tick-boxes)
