[[Zettelkasten and Evergreen MOC]] 

Copy Paste is too easy. Especially worse (or tired?) students are inclined to just copy paste information, which leads to shallow reading.

Copy Paste is convenient, but any information *not* selected is more likely to be ignored [[Von Restorff effect]], this might lead to copy everything and the kitchen–sync, which might *also* not work well.

Best would be limiting the information copied. How to limit oneself is not something I have figures out yet.

Highlighting in Zotero and Calibre, which has color-highlighting [[Colors and taxonomies]], has the extra step od deciding what color to use (classify the highlight). This extra slowdown helps me to read more carefully. Kindle highlighting is too easy to overdo.

## Literature notes 
[[@Exploring Differences in Students' Copy-and-Paste Decision Making and Processing: A Mixed-Methods Study]] 
Unlimited CP results in worse engagement, and study results
- highlighting without review is ludicrous
- predicted bad result if CP is not restricted in some way
	- this proved to be true
		- clearly visible that students engaged less with the material if not needed
- Restricting CP had a positive influence on how careful students read and thought about the material
