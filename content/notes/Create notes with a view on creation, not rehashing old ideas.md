[[Zettelkasten and Evergreen MOC]] #engagement → #emotion

When creating literature notes, try to take note of the **emotion** specific passages called up. If something was surprising, make a note of that. Over time the surprise will wane, but the emotion can be used in the future when the note is used as *source for an external work.*

>[!note] This is surprisingly hard

We were initially surprised (or outraged), or felt any other emotion that made us take note of the idea we met. When writing a new note, **create notes with a view on creation**, not just rehashing old ideas. This immediately will change the focus of the note. *What* makes this not special for me? *Why* do I like it, or hate it? Don’t build wikipedia [[don't quote]].

It helps to link notes from the [[daily note]] when we first had / found the idea. It gives additional context.
