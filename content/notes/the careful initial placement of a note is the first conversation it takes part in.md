Careful placement in [[Keyword Index]] gives *enough friction* to force one to slow down and *carefully think*. Where does this go in the grand scheme of things? This results in better connections.

[[Classical zettelkasten was a balanced but outdated system|Classical zetttelkasten]] makes having an overview less convenient but having one large outline (that can be folded) is by definition build for this

The first conversation is a good start, follow keywords to create an initial conversation between the notes.

This would be a start on re-reading on the use of the #folgezettel. [[note sequences are the key to ZK, they create clusters that birth new ideas]]

Modern [[pkm|pkms]] don’t really need locations anymore. Every note can be connected to every other note. Still, the initial friction helps to reduce the blind collecting of useless notes. [[collectors fallacy]]

## Fleeting notes 
- Notes need to be somewhere, even in the mechanistic view. Logical to put them somewhere "logical", instead of alphabetical, or just sorted by date
	- the sequence helps to think about the whole. It shouldn't be just a matter of dumping it wherever.
		- It gives direction to thought, so should be an informed decision: where do I want to go?
- Collectors fallacy is real, but placing a note in the hope it will be helpful in the future an-sich is not that
	- linked to the previous point, the sequence is not a shot in the dark, serendipity based hoping-for-the-best. It is an informed placement
- Birds-eye view of the whole
	- dated naming scheme is one way of doing things
		- ugly AF
	- The goal is to be able to see things -> master structure zettel is preferable, and possible in this day and age
- Initial placement of a note might not be where it is most useful (key-argument from S), but it is (should be) still a good start
	- It gives a place in the whole, which was the basic idea. But it also help to consider other (your own) viewpoints. Why here?
		- Even if you use it elsewhere, it doesn't make your initial reasoning a waste.
						- every permanent note is a (potential) structure zettel
