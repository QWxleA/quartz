[[Zettelkasten and Evergreen MOC]] #productivity

**Definition:** systematic actions that lead to a preset goal

A workflow is a clearly defined goal and outcome, along with the steps required to obtain certain results.

[[flow|Flow]] is achieved when an action becomes automatic, effortless. When a workflow becomes automatic, because the **simple** steps have become a [[habit|habits]], that will make it feel as if the work gets done without effort. 

To reach this goal, the separate steps have to be simple, [[automatic notes|possibly automated]], and can be done without conscious thought. Then all the mental effort can be used for the actual task, not the processes surrounding it.

**Goal of a workflow (general definition):** Increasing #productivity, reducing costs, becoming more #agile, and improving information exchange

## (Primary) Sources
A workflow consists of an orchestrated and repeatable pattern of activity, enabled by the systematic organization of resources into processes that transform materials, provide services, or process information. — [Workflow - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workflow)
