## Andy -- A reading inbox to capture possibly-useful references

Have an Inbox Zero approach to inputs. Don't let tabs, or PDFs gather. Use a GTD-style Inbox where everything is gathered. The reading inbox is triaged thusly:

1.  Trashed -- not worth the time
2.  Read seriously (i.e. write about what you read)
3.  Look over and file in the reference library
4.  Store for later use (useful, but not timely)

The goal is to [[Close open loops]], don't let things gather.
