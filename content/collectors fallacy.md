[[🪤Human behavior as automatons]] 

> Because ‘to know about something’ isn’t the same as ‘knowing something’ — [[Christian]], The Collector’s Fallacy

**collectors fallacy**: The tendency to ==gather useful stuff, and feel good== about it. It's a fallacy (the use of invalid or otherwise faulty reasoning) because it gives the mistaken idea of an increase in knowledge and understanding
- It makes us _feel_ good -> addictive
- collecting is not learning
- understanding -> how does this piece of information relate to other information in my graph -> [[linking]]
- truly understanding something means you can explain it to others who are not proficient in that field
