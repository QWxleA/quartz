What is rhizomatic linking?

---

 In the context of **note-taking**, a **rhizome** refers to a **non-linear, interconnected structure** for organizing ideas—drawing on the metaphor from botany and philosophy. 

![[pub_rhizome theory.png|500]]

The point of a [[zettelkasten]] is to link notes in a non-linear network to other notes. This is per definition rhizomatic. Every note can be anywhere in a thread of notes. This creates a rich tapestry of original (for us) thought.

Fundamental  is that notes are [[atomic note|atomic]]

See: [Rhizome (philosophy) - Wikiwand](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Rhizome_(philosophy))
> Deleuze and Guattari use the terms "**rhizome** and **rhizomatic** (from Ancient Greek ῥίζωμα, _rhízōma_, "mass of roots") to describe a network that "connects any point to any other point".
