[[Exploring Andy's notes]] 
## Andy - Daily working log
Create a new note every day, so there is a place to put things down. This is the lowest level of note, "ephemeral by design". If a note is good enough, raise it to the [[Writing Inbox]]. This space might even be used for drafting [[Notes/evergreen notes]].

- The goal of the daily page is [[Close open loops]], a no-friction area to empty ones head
- Anything worthwile should be *promoted* up the [[Note Taxonomy]] ladder
## Me
This works well, I journal >300 days a year. [[Use daily notes to close open loops and keep a journal]]
