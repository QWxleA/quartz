[[⏱️Being productive and healthy]] #personality_type → #success 

A fitting (as in a glove) work environment will make it easier to get into [[flow]]. The tools we are using should not disturb us from doing our work. This can go both ways. A system can be not good enough, which would be annoying, or it is too fiddly, and instead of note-taking we spend time *improving* our system. (→ [[friction]])

A [[zettelkasten]] is an example of a good tool. It helps collect and (re-)find information, but it also gives freedom to follow ones fancy. Thus being both liberating *and* structured at the same time.
