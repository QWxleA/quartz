What makes links more valuable than the notes they link together? #flashcard 

The main *purpose* of a ZK is to link separate [[iteration#^valuablelinks|small]] notes together [[there is understanding in the combination of ideas]]. If two notes linked together are valuable, a whole string of notes linked together even more so. They form a line of thought, or more practical, a possible outline. Over time individual elements of the string might become outdated. The notes coming before and after, though, can still be valid, so the string is still intact.

---
For this to work we should *never* remove notes. It will break links. Just update, and or invalidate a note. The note can still work as a spring-board. Maybe as a time-machine to see our old thinking. [[Use daily notes to close open loops and keep a journal]] and see a time capsule of what happened then.

The product of the links is **understanding** how concepts and ideas relate. The **goal is not having more notes**.

## Fleeting notes

**A note is like a moment in time, a current understanding of a subject.**
- The links show how a specific fact fits into a web of thought. As thoughts are highly temporal links are a snapshot of the current understanding.

**links are more stable and important than (the contents of) notes.**
- the goal was a birds-eye view, not the collection of notes
	- notes are temporary, the overall structure permanent
- results are highly temporal, as the results are shown "in the moment". Later the [[graph]] will have changed

links are important yet ethereal, the snapshot is real
- links are more important than the actual note.
	- The note might get outdated, but the links to and from the note stay
		- It's like a memory about a person you no longer know, but the memories stay
			- and the events that followed also stay
- Notes are not meant to be copied from
	- they are meant to help think about a subject
		- thinking should be about the links and the concepts, not necessarily the text of the notes
			- this needs more thought
- The state of the links is in constant flux
	- this makes every interaction unique
		- this in itself adds [[entropy]]
- What is "connections get actualized?"
	- when using the graph, we make notes of the current state of the link
		- those links show the opinion about the issue
			- these are the bullet-points of the work
