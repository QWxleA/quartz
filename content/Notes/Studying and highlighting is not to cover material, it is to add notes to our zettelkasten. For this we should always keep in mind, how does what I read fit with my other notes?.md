[[Zettelkasten and Evergreen notes]] 

When we read we should bring to mind note related to what we are currently reading. Don't read just anything, it will make it hard to add to you slip-box.

After we found where approximately the new note should fit we can search for things to add to it in the publication we consume. [[Questions help us to actively search instead of passively receive (information)]]
